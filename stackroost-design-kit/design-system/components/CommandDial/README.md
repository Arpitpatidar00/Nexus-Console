The quick-switch dial from the radial-menu reference: a glass ring of module segments around an ivory knob, with the modules fanned out as chips.

- Opens with ⌘K (or the knob). Arrow keys or clicking a segment pick a module; typing switches to the palette list (see layouts).
- The active segment is `ember-soft` with an ember edge; its chip gets the ember border.
- 6–8 segments. More modules than that: the dial shows pinned ones, the palette shows all.
- It is navigation, not a place for destructive actions.
