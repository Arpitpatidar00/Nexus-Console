The Stackroost mark: three stacked pills (your stack) with a warm dot roosting on top.

- Pills take `foreground` at 45 / 72 / 100 % opacity; the dot takes `ember`. Below 48 px the halo ring is dropped and the dot grows.
- App icon: `assets/Logos/stackroost-app-icon.svg` (smoked glass squircle). Alternative: `stackroost-app-icon-dial.svg` (the ivory dial knob).
- Menu bar: `stackroost-menubar-template.svg` is a black-only template image. macOS recolours it; never ship a coloured menu-bar icon.
- Do not rotate, outline, add gradients, or put the mark on ember.
